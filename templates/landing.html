How it works

Three steps and about ten seconds of setup.

1

Drop in a playlist

Paste any public Spotify playlist, or use the demo mix. Moodify looks up each song's Last.fm tags and sorts it into a mood.

2

Turn on your camera

A small face-expression model runs right in your browser and reads your mood every second.

3

Press play

Matching songs show up as Spotify players. When your mood changes, the picks change too. Save any set to your Spotify account.
